ADR-0231 Integration-only K=1 MJWarp collector CUDA graph contract¶

语言: English

  • Status: Rejected / no-go at M2 / historical pre-registration

  • Date: 2026-10-13; #2311 addendum 2026-10-14; closeout 2026-10-15

  • Owners: UniLab runtime, UniSim MJWarp backend, uni_rl off-policy collector

  • Roadmaps: #2307 (historical contract), #2311 (M0 boundary addendum)

  • Scope: historical pre-registration and integration evidence; no active runtime contract

  • Supersedes: None

  • Superseded by: None. ADR-0238 was subsequently rejected in #2358; its residual public lifecycle/completion naming was removed by #2359.

Context¶

The K=1 off-policy collector has a strict transition dependency: observation[t] -> learner action[t] -> env.step(action[t]) -> observation[t+1]. Phase A profiling on the canonical MJWarp / FlashSAC / G1 motion workload found addressable collector overhead of 19.54% in independent-context mode and 32.71% under MPS. The dominant blockers are manager update and episode reset dispatch, hot validation reductions, dynamic reset-row compaction, and reset-path temporary allocation. MJWarp already owns fixed-shape physics step/reset graphs, but that does not cover Manager-based observations, rewards, termination, events, commands, packed reset, or transition publication.

This is not a production commitment, no release artifact is published, and promotion to main remains an owner decision outside #2307.

The owner decision is now final: #2311 closed no-go at M2. Three successive owner-first prototypes showed that nested MJWarp graph replay is prohibited during caller capture and that the non-nested public path reaches transient MuJoCo-Warp allocation/free that invalidates the outer capture. This ADR is retained as historical pre-registration and audit evidence only. It is not an active execution plan, does not authorize collector_graph in UniLab, and makes no support or performance claim. Its derivative state-update-only route was also rejected; it is not an active successor boundary. The surviving eager receipt boundary is operation-neutral completion metadata, not a graph lifecycle or whole-collector tick contract.

Issue #2307 closed at Gate B no-go only because its Phase B boundary could not remove the then-private backend/replay completion barriers. Issue #2309 has since delivered and released those public contracts. This #2311 addendum therefore fixes the present boundary without reopening either historical result: the first implementation captures the fixed Manager collector publication subboundary, not merely MJWarp physics, and not yet the complete manager lifecycle for every task. The distinction is binding: the subboundary still owns action/control transform, physics, all Manager observations and rewards used by the canonical transition, termination, reset flags, packed reset, post-reset observations, transition publication, and replay ingress. It excludes only owner-declared semantic publications that preflight rejects for this workload (interval events, metric/curriculum/recorder terms, manual reset, and mapped multi-entity reset layouts), plus policy inference and learner updates which must never enter this graph.

Decision¶

The decision recorded here was pre-registered for the #2311 integration branch and was rejected at M2 before promotion to main. The sections below preserve the exact historical boundary, inventory, mode, workload, and gates for audit; they must not be read as pending M1/M2 work. Historical API/config/test names below no longer exist; #2358 also removed the derivative caller-owned update route. The neutral runtime hot-path audit that survived that removal was itself deleted under #2363 in favor of observable-contract tests.

Define an integration-only public capture boundary negotiated through SimBackend capabilities. The candidate consumes one preallocated action input of shape [num_envs, action_dim] and produces fixed-shape observation, reward, terminated, truncated, terminal-observation, and transition-publication carriers. The candidate is exactly K=1; policy inference and learner updates are outside capture.

Public capability and preflight¶

SimBackend exposes a public capture candidate capability and preflight contract before any environment graph is constructed. Preflight requires fixed shapes/devices, declared stable buffers, supported Warp/Torch stream semantics, allocator readiness, RNG ownership, and capability support. Unsupported capabilities fail closed; UniLab never probes or branches on backend-private attributes.

For #2311 the public surface was the existing contract version 1: declared SimBackend capabilities plus an env-side candidate preflight that layered on the owner carriers and semantic-owner rejection list, so no additional backend contract was required before M1. The neutral uni_rl completion protocol, receipt gating, replay ingress order/capture identity, and manifest schema were likewise judged sufficient for the first publication handoff. An owner-controlled mode negotiation protocol was to be added in UniLab before graph execution; until then graph remained disabled and this preflight was evidence only. The graph-candidate preflight, graph-completion publication, and graph lifecycle/invalidation APIs named in the historical record were deleted by the collector-graph removal (see Evidence In Repo).

Buffer and stream ownership¶

  • UniLab policy/inference IPC owns action publication and signals action readiness to the collector graph stream.

  • The collector graph owns in-graph action/control reads and all fixed output carriers.

  • Warp physics and Torch manager kernels execute on the declared graph stream with explicit events at stream ownership transitions.

  • Replay ingress owns fixed transition slots and consumes a graph-completion event; it may overlap only after that event and must preserve contiguous tick order.

  • Learner sampling and policy inference never access in-flight carriers.

  • Independent-context and MPS execution use the same ordering contract; MPS changes execution sharing, not semantics.

#2311 M0 fixed carrier layout (historical evidence)¶

All addresses are fixed before preflight/capture and live on the selected CUDA device unless explicitly marked CPU:

  • action input: float32[N,29];

  • control: float32[N,29];

  • observations: float32[N,160] actor and float32[N,289] critic;

  • reward: float32[N];

  • reward components: float32[N,11];

  • terminated/truncated/reset mask: bool[N] each;

  • terminal observation and post-reset observation groups: one float32[N,dim] carrier for each observation group;

  • validation verdicts: persistent scalar/full-width device bool carriers named by their owners;

  • transition publication: full-width obs, critic, next-obs, next-critic, action, reward, done, truncated, terminal mask, and terminal observation columns copied into replay-owned fixed ingress slots (two slots of N rows);

  • replay ingress handoff: ordered step receipt, active non-empty capture identity, monotonically nondecreasing sequence, stream identity, and ingress publication event;

  • RNG evidence: CPU state tensor plus device label, monotonic state version and draw version.

The final semantic/performance parity consumer must add comparison-only clones outside capture; those copies are evidence carriers, not candidate inputs or outputs.

#2311 pre-registered gates (historical evidence)¶

These gates were pre-registered before implementation. Gate S was never reached because real collector graph execution failed structurally at M2. Gate P was therefore never run, and no throughput number from this route may be cited.

Gate S requires eager/graph equality over observations by group, rewards and all components, terminated/truncated, episode and reset counters, actions/commands, recorder trace, reward means, curriculum state, metrics, terminal/reset/post-reset observations, public qpos/qvel, RNG state and versions, lifecycle snapshot and capture identity, transition layout/order, replay ingress commit order, failed replay no-transition rollback, invalidation/fallback behavior, empty/partial/full masks, contiguous final-observation publication, equal replay publish/release counters, no stale/reordered transitions, matching capture identity, and fail-closed malformed/unsupported diagnostics. It runs on null and MPS with eager, graph, and capture-warmup/replay trajectories and requires all three static audits zero.

Gate P uses fixed null and MPS arms, three fresh-process paired repeats per mode, interleaved graph/eager, 300 iterations, and the last-100 aggregate median of run_summary.json:tail_env_steps_per_sec. Graph passes only if at least one arm improves by at least +10% and every safety observation holds. A failing arm must fail closed or explicitly fall back with a manifest reason; one passing mode does not authorize another. Throughput can never override Gate S. No threshold, shape, K, semantic surface, or mode rule may change after results are observed.

Lifecycle and invalidation¶

The lifecycle is: allocate persistent carriers, eager warmup, preflight, capture, replay, completion publication, invalidation, fallback/fail-closed. Graph state, contract version, disable reason, fallback count, replay ordering counters, and stream/RNG evidence enter the run manifest. Capture is invalidated on model/data/view/capacity/policy-I/O change, backend graph invalidation, or stream/event failure.

The historical integration branch proposed an owner-selected cold-path mode key collector_graph (disabled | eager | graph): graph could be requested only after the public preflight verdict succeeded, unsupported/malformed states failed closed, and runtime fallback was explicit, counted, reason-bearing, and never silently enabled another mode. Mode never changed inside a control step. The mode key, its API, and its manifest surface are intentionally abandoned: they did not reach UniLab main, were fully deleted by the #2363 collector-graph removal, and must not be reintroduced through derivative branches or reused or reinterpreted by ADR-0238’s env_update_graph.

#2311 workload fixation (historical evidence)¶

The canonical frozen workload is FlashSAC / G1 motion tracking / MJWarp with: N=2048; action [2048,29]; observation groups actor/critic [2048,160] and [2048,289]; the eleven canonical motion/action/contact reward components; terminated, truncated, and reset masks [2048]; seed 1 (one ManagerRng generator); no interval events, metric, curriculum, recorder, manual-reset, or mapped multi-entity reset owners; training.env_steps_per_sync=1; inference_slot_capacity=1; FlashSAC n_step=1; replay capacity 512*2048 rows; ingress depth 2; ingress slot rows 2048.

The benchmark runs were fixed as fresh-process, same-head null and MPS arms of the canonical train command (uv run --no-sync train --algo flashsac --task g1_motion_tracking --sim mjwarp algo.seed=1 env.seed=1 algo.max_iterations=300 training.no_play=true) with the now-deleted mode key swept over disabled|eager|graph, plus the #2311-prescribed paired repeat/interleave orchestration. They are retained as historical evidence, not as runnable support or a pending benchmark plan. The Manager RNG has its own env.seed; algo.seed alone does not fix it. Every run records the resolved seed. Graph and eager must not be mixed within one distribution. The recorded host is GPU-db468bab-1b15-2a0b-b5e6-7716e512d422 (RTX 4090), driver 580.178.04, CUDA runtime 13.0, PyTorch 2.14.0+cu130, Warp 1.16.0, MuJoCo/Warp 3.11.0; each final gate run must re-record exact values rather than relying on this M0 snapshot.

The frozen RNG/draw order is: the initial randomized episode-length draw during init_state; then on each reset joint-default bias, resampling time, adaptive motion sampler (multinomial bins then uniform offsets), pose, velocity, and joint noise; then actor observation noise during observation compute. A failed or invalidated replay restores the complete pre-boundary generator state and never advances this order silently.

Validation and semantics¶

Hot finite/shape checks become device-resident fixed packs. A failed device verdict fails graph completion; exact host diagnostics run at the failure boundary. Reward logs, episode metrics, termination diagnostics, reset counts, and other semantic publications move to interval/completion boundaries without deletion. Final observation, terminal transition ordering, and post-reset observation semantics remain identical to eager execution.

Torch RNG state is consumed in a deterministic capture-safe order. RNG state/offset is versioned; replay or invalidation resumes from the same recorded state and never silently advances consumption.

Fallback policy¶

Malformed manifests or unsupported capabilities fail closed before capture. Runtime graph failure permits explicit eager fallback only when no transition was published for the failed replay and the manifest records reason, counts, and deterministic state rollback. Otherwise execution fails closed.

K=1 boundary¶

K>1, pre-committed action sequences, stale/replay inference, and speculative transitions are outside this contract. FlashSAC n_step=1 is TD bootstrapping, not action-block K.

Consequences¶

  • The complete K=1 collector graph route is rejected. Its independently useful eager carrier, public lifecycle, deferred-verdict, and correctness work is preserved on main; collector graph execution itself remains unsupported.

  • collector_graph API, defaults, manifests, resolver behavior, and training integration are not public contracts and must not be merged from the branch.

  • ADR-0238’s derivative state-update-only route was also rejected in #2358; no caller-owned collector/update graph boundary remains active.

  • No graph-enabled support or performance claim follows from this historical record. The structural M2 evidence and the absence of Gate S/Gate P runs are the relevant outcomes.

  • Backend capability negotiation remains public through unisim.backend.base.SimBackend; backend-private probing is prohibited.

  • Manager-Based training semantics, reset masks, terminal observations, transition ordering, semantic logs, and episode statistics remain eager-equivalent.

  • Policy inference, learner updates, K>1 action blocks, pre-committed action sequences, and stale/replay inference remain outside capture.

  • Manifests fail closed and expose graph version/state/reason/fallback counters.

  • UniLab package version remains 1.3.5; no UniLab tag, PyPI publication, or GitHub release is permitted while #2311 is open, and no graph-enabled support claim may be made before both pre-registered gates pass.

Alternatives Considered¶

  • Expand MJWarp physics graphs only: rejected because Phase A shows Manager update/reset and publication overhead outside the existing physics graph.

  • Capture the learner update cycle too: rejected because policy/model updates require outside-graph ownership and would violate the bounded collector roadmap.

  • No complete collector graph route: selected by the M2 structural no-go; nested graph replay was prohibited and the public composition path encountered capture-invalidating transient allocation/free.
